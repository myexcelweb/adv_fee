Note on the scale Article 1 (Schedule I) is worded in steps, but the Act's own "Table of rates of ad-valorem fees" gives different amounts below ₹ 1 lakh (e.g. ₹ 5,950 at ₹ 1,00,000), while the text steps agree with the table above ₹ 1 lakh. This calculator follows the Act's table and its stated rates (₹ 200 per ₹ 10,000 up to ₹ 10 lakh, ₹ 1,200 per lakh up to ₹ 20 lakh, ₹ 500 per lakh beyond; maximum ₹ 75,000). The table prints ₹ 10 for values up to ₹ 1,000, which breaks the ₹ 100-per-₹ 1,000 pattern; ₹ 100 is used here. Confirm with the Court's fee office for very small claims.

Not covered

Fixed fees (Schedule II), refunds (Sections 15–17, 43), area-based declaration fees (Section 6(5)) and periodical-charge valuations (Section 6(2), (8)) are not calculated. Companies Act proceedings (Rule 444), Rule X commission fees, and separate-set or apportioned costs (Rule 443) need Court directions.
